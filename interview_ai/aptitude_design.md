# Aptitude AI Module – Zecpath

## Objective

Evaluate candidate logical thinking, reasoning ability and situational judgment.

## Components

### Question Engine
- Logical reasoning questions
- Situational judgment questions
- Analytical thinking questions

### Answer Understanding Engine
- Detect reasoning steps
- Detect structured thinking
- Detect decision patterns

### Scoring Engine
- Logical correctness
- Problem solving clarity
- Decision quality

### Scenario Evaluator
- Compare answer with ideal patterns
- Evaluate effectiveness

### Output
- Aptitude score (0-100)
- Breakdown
- Strengths and gaps
